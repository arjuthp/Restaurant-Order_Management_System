## Design Principles Applied

1. **Minimal Green Usage**
   - Green for headings, borders, and accents only
   - No overwhelming green backgrounds
   - Strategic placement

2. **Bold Typography**
   - Headings: 700 weight
   - Subheadings: 600 weight
   - Clear hierarchy

3. **Clean Backgrounds**
   - White for cards and content
   - Light gray for pages
   - No busy gradients

4. **Consistent Styling**
   - Admin and customer views match
   - Same color theory throughout
   - Professional appearance

5. **Better Contrast**
   - Dark text on light backgrounds
   - High readability
   - Accessible design
